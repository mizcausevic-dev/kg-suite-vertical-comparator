## How to use this comparison

**Buyer's procurement team**: Use the 6 × 6 matrix to understand which Suite shapes apply to your AI vendor portfolio across mixed regulated verticals. The same procurement Decision Card pattern works for a HealthTech AI vendor AND a FinTech AI vendor — the vault-contract data categories + consent_basis taxonomy differ, but the shape is identical.

**Compliance counsel**: Use the cross-cutting invariants table to map equivalent obligations across regulated lines of business — when your firm advises clients across multiple verticals, the four-fifths-rule (PropTech, InsurTech, HR Tech, FinTech) and human-in-loop (HealthTech, PropTech, InsurTech, HR Tech, FinTech) patterns transfer with vertical-specific scoping rules.

**AI vendor**: Use the per-shape matrix to identify the right Suite spec your product should target. If you're an AI vendor selling into FinTech, your Decision Card should conform to `financial-customer-data-vault-contract-v0.1`. If you sell into both InsurTech AND FinTech, you produce two Decision Cards using the same shape with the right vertical-specific profile.

**Standards observer**: The matrix is a longitudinal record of how AI-governance design patterns transfer across regulated verticals — and where each vertical contributes a novel pattern (e.g. InsurTech's `actuarial-soundness-defended`, HR Tech's two-orthogonal-invariants audit stream, FinTech's institution-type-aware regulator routing).
